## 2026-08-28 — Phase 0 shortcut and leakage audit

**Command**

```bash
python -m flowconx.audit.run_audit \
  --csv data/processed/flowconx_final_labeled_train.csv \
  --seed 42 --rare-class-mode drop
```

**Commit:** see `results/audit/flowconx_final_labeled_train/audit_summary.json`
→ `environment.git_commit`
**Dataset:** 112,098 rows after dropping `xr_interactive` (23 rows, below
`min_class_count=100`); 5 classes.
**Source files:** `results/audit/flowconx_final_labeled_train/*/baselines.json`,
`results/audit/flowconx_final_labeled_train/*/leakage.json`

**Headline numbers** (macro-F1; full table in

| Baseline | `random_flow` | `app_disjoint` |

| AppScanner (2016), RF | **0.8690** | 0.2018 |
| FlowCon-X, recorded (not reproducible, different partition) | *0.8842* | TODO |

`session_disjoint`, `temporal`, `server_disjoint` and `origin_disjoint` are
**unavailable**: the committed CSV retains no provenance columns. The preparers
now emit them; the dataset must be regenerated.

**Leakage:** `random_flow` fails on exact duplicates (53 test rows), near
duplicates (21.3% of sampled test at cosine ≥ 0.999) and nuisance derivability
(`condition` reconstructed from two model inputs with agreement 1.000).
`app_disjoint` fails the latter two (2.1%, 1.000).

**What surprised me:** AppScanner, a 2016 statistical baseline, lands within 1.5
macro-F1 points of the recorded FlowCon-X number on the same protocol, and five
NetFlow scalars recover two thirds of the task. Under `app_disjoint` every
baseline collapses by 0.5-0.7 macro-F1. See `AUDIT.md` §8.

**Status:** complete. Phase 0 checkpoint reached; awaiting a decision on
replanning before Phase 1.
